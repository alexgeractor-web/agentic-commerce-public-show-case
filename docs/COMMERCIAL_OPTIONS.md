## Delivery safeguards

1. Review the merchant stack, business requirements and availability of a native alternative.
2. Confirm feasible scope, assumptions, exclusions, applicable regulations and engineering capacity.
3. Agree a **written statement of work** before payment: deliverables, milestones, price, dependencies, acceptance tests, defect correction, support period and intellectual-property terms.
4. Use authorized synthetic or sandbox data before considering live merchant information. Never exchange production credentials through public Issues or unprotected messages.
5. Document test evidence and remaining risks, and agree explicit handover / acceptance.
